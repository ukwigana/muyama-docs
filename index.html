Privacy Policy for Muyama

Last Updated: February 26, 2026

Ukwigana operates the Muyama mobile application. This policy informs you of our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Muyama is a "privacy-first" application. Your financial data is stored locally on your device using the Isar Database.

  • Financial Data: Income, expenses, and goals are stored locally and are not transmitted to our servers.
  • Permissions: The app may request access to your Camera/Storage to attach receipt photos to transactions.

2. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services that collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase (Optional for Cloud Sync)

3. Security

We value your trust in providing us your personal information. Since data is stored locally, we recommend using device-level security (PIN, Biometrics) to protect your data.
